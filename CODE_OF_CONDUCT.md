# Code of Conduct

Outbound OS is built by people who sell for a living and people who write
software, often in different countries and first languages. Be the kind of
collaborator you would want on a hard deal.

## Expected

- Be patient and assume good intent, especially with newcomers and people
  writing in a second language.
- Critique code and ideas, not people. Say what you would do instead.
- Credit other people's work.
- Accept a maintainer's decision on scope gracefully; forks are welcome.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information — including customer or lead data
  from your own install — in issues, logs or screenshots.
- Using this project or its community to send spam.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainers through
[a private security advisory](https://github.com/thatsarpit/outbound-os/security/advisories/new)
(it reaches only the maintainers), marked "conduct". Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads, or ban
participants who break these rules.

This code is adapted in spirit from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
